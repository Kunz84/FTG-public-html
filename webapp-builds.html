Common Questions

Answers Before You Ask

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. Every build includes an owner admin dashboard where you manage products, prices, photos, content, orders and customers — no developer needed for everyday changes.

Can customers pay with Apple Pay or monthly payments?

Yes. Secure checkout through Stripe shows Apple Pay, Link and other wallets automatically, and Affirm pay-over-time messaging can appear on eligible products.

Do customers need an account to buy?

No. Guest checkout is built in. Customers who do create an account get saved addresses and order history.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Every site is designed for phones first, then polished for tablets and desktops.

Can you move my content from Facebook or eBay?

Yes. We bring over your products, photos, videos and reviews so your new site launches with your real content.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Your site launches on your own web address with a secure connection.

Can you connect my business tools to AI?

Yes. We set up connections that let AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and Perplexity work with your business tools — the same kind of connection we run in our own business today.

Do you handle shipping?

We build custom shipping rules around how you ship today. Live carrier rates can be scoped for your build.

How much does it cost?

Every build is different. Tell us what you want to build and David will walk through options with you directly.
