What We Built

A Complete Online Store the Owner Runs Day to Day

JB Performance needed more than a storefront. Customers shop by bike, so the site is organized around fitment for the LC4 platform, with product pages, add-on suggestions and secure checkout that takes cards, Apple Pay and other wallets. Buyers can check out as guests or create an account to track orders. Dyno results, videos, photos and customer reviews show the parts' performance, and every order and message lands in an admin dashboard where the owner updates products, content and orders without a developer.

Inside the Build

Everything JB Performance Runs On

Six parts of the build, all live on the shop's own domain.

Online Shop & Secure Checkout

  • Shop by bike: fitment for KTM 690, Husqvarna 701 and GasGas 700
  • Categories, filters, keyword search and fitment badges
  • Product pages with galleries, related products and add-on suggestions
  • Secure card checkout through Stripe, with Apple Pay, Link and wallets
  • Affirm pay-over-time messaging on eligible products
  • Inventory tracking, bundles and shipping rules

Customer Accounts & Orders

  • Create account, email verification, login and forgot password
  • Saved billing and shipping addresses
  • Order history and tracking in one place
  • Guest checkout, with no account required to buy
  • Privacy requests: data export and account closure

Automatic Emails

  • Order confirmation, payment received, and shipped with tracking
  • Account emails: verification, password reset and security alerts
  • Owner alerts for every new order and new contact message
  • Each email sends once, with no duplicates

Results, Media & Content

  • Dyno results with interactive before-and-after charts
  • Photo gallery with full-screen viewer and captions
  • Video library with hosted playback and YouTube/Shorts embeds
  • Blog with categories and tags
  • Customer reviews with star ratings and source attribution
  • An About page that tells the shop's story

Owner Admin Dashboard

  • At-a-glance badges for new orders and messages
  • Manage products, photos, content, orders and customers
  • Contact inbox with message status tracking
  • Staff invitations with an approval step
  • Site settings: business info, hours, social links, shipping and payment options

Mobile, Fast & Search Ready

  • Designed for phones first, polished on every screen
  • Fast first load on the shop's own domain, with a secure connection
  • Page titles, descriptions and sharing previews for search engines
  • Card details never touch the site; Stripe handles them
