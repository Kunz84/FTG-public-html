How & When You Get Paid

Commissions are paid on Net-30 terms — the standard payout schedule built into our affiliate platform. Here's exactly how the money flows.

A sale is made. Someone clicks your link and buys the Launchpad or subscribes to the Builders Club. It's tracked to your affiliate ID instantly and shows in your dashboard.

Every commission is held ("Pending") until 30 days after the end of the month it was earned in — then it's released to you.

Example: a sale on July 10 is earned in July. July ends on the 31st, the 30-day clock runs through August 30, and your commission releases at the end of August.

Recurring works the same way: each monthly Builders Club payment starts its own Net-30 clock when collected — a steady rolling income stream for as long as your referral stays subscribed.

Fair and transparent: if a member refunds while a commission is still pending, it's voided — you're never paid on returned money. Everything already released is yours to keep.

Recurring commissions compound. If a Builders Club member you referred stays active, you earn 15% of their subscription every month they remain subscribed — up to 12 months. One $97/mo referral can be worth up to $194 across the first year.

Affiliate FAQ

How long does my affiliate cookie last?
365 days. If someone clicks your link today and buys 11 months from now, you still get credit — as long as they don't click another affiliate's link in the meantime. This is one of the longest cookies in the industry.
What happens if a customer refunds?
If a refund is issued while the commission is still pending, it's voided before it pays out. If a refund happens after you've been paid, it's deducted from your next payout. This is standard and protects against refund abuse.
Can I promote through paid ads?
Yes, with prior approval. Reach out in the affiliate community or email affiliates@fasttrackgenai.com with your ad copy, landing page, and platform. We review within 48 hours. Unapproved paid ads violate the terms and may result in suspension.
Do I earn on upsells and recurring subscriptions?
Yes, across the board. You earn your tier rate on every product a referred customer buys — Launchpad (OTO or standard), the 3-Month or 6-Month Passes, and the Builders Club initial + 15% recurring for up to 12 months. One referral can generate $190+ in lifetime value.
How do my commission rates rise?
Your rate rises with total sales volume: 25% on your first 10 sales, 30% from 11–50, and 35% at 51+. There are no gates — every approved affiliate earns on both products from day one; the rate simply grows as your volume does.
What promotional materials do I get?
Your dashboard Resources section includes email swipe copy, social post templates (LinkedIn, X, Facebook), a demo clip library, image assets, and a "Quick Start" script you can record as a Loom or voice note. Everything is editable — customize it in your voice.
Can I promote to my email list or community?
Absolutely — email, Slack, Discord, private communities, LinkedIn, X. If it's your audience and you're not spamming strangers, you're good. Just make sure any promotion accurately represents the product and doesn't make income claims we don't support.
