How & When You Get Paid

Commissions are paid on Net-30 terms — the standard payout schedule built into our affiliate platform.

Why the wait? This window clears our 30-day money-back guarantee before a penny is paid, so you're only ever paid on sales that truly stick. Here's exactly how it works:

Every commission is held ("Pending") until 30 days after the end of the month it was earned in — then it's released to you.

Example: A sale on July 10 is earned in July. July ends on the 31st, the 30-day clock runs through August 30, and your commission releases at the end of August.

In practice that's a 30–60 day wait from the sale date — protecting you from chargebacks and refunds so your earnings are secure once released.

Recurring works the same way: each monthly Builders Club payment starts its own Net-30 clock when collected — a steady rolling income stream for as long as your referral stays subscribed.

Fair and transparent: if a member refunds while a commission is still pending, it's voided (you're never paid on returned money). Everything already released is yours to keep.

Common Questions

What's the difference between OTO and Standard pricing?
OTO (One-Time Offer) pricing is what people see when they click your link and buy right away — $37 Launchpad, $47/mo Builders Club. People who don't buy immediately enter our nurture sequence and later see Standard pricing ($97 / $97/mo). Your 365-day cookie tracks both, and Standard sales actually earn you a higher commission.
What is the Business Accelerator — do I promote it separately?
No. The Business Accelerator is a 7-agent AI workforce ($731 value) included free inside every Builders Club membership during launch. It's a bonus that makes the Club an easy "yes" — not a separate product. You earn your commissions on the Builders Club pricing, which already includes it.
How do the recurring commissions work?
For every Builders Club member you refer, you earn your tier % on the first month (or pass), then a flat 15% on each monthly payment after — for up to 12 months total. If they stay the full year you earn the initial plus the recurring; if they cancel in month 4, recurring stops at month 4. You're only ever paid on payments actually collected.
Why is my commission held for 30 days?
Net-30 clears our 30-day money-back guarantee window. It protects you from clawbacks — you're only ever paid on sales that stick. Once a commission is released (30–60 days after the sale), it's yours permanently. No surprises, no reversals.
Someone clicked my link but bought 6 months later — do I still get paid?
Yes. Your 365-day cookie means you get the credit for any purchase within a full year of the click. They'll likely see Standard pricing by then, which earns you the higher commission. Our nurture sequence does that selling for you — no extra work required.
Can I promote both Launchpad and Builders Club?
Yes — and you should. Most affiliates start with the Launchpad (low $37 barrier) to get first sales, then lean into the Builders Club, which is where recurring income compounds. One link covers the whole funnel, so you earn on both automatically.
Do performance bonuses stack with commissions?
Yes. Bonuses are paid on top of your regular commissions. Hit 5, 10, and 25 total sales and that's an extra $25, $100, and $250 — $375 in bonus cash on top of everything you've already earned.
