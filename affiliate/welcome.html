How & When You Get Paid

We run on a Net-30 payout schedule with a $50 minimum threshold. Here's exactly how the money flows:

1

A Sale Is Made

Someone clicks your link, buys Business Launchpad or subscribes to Builders Club. The sale is instantly tracked to your affiliate ID and shows in your dashboard.

2

30-Day Holding Period

The commission enters a 30-day hold to account for refunds and payment disputes. This protects both you and us from clawbacks.

3

Commission Becomes Payable

After the hold period clears, your commission moves to "Approved" status. If your total approved balance is $50 or more, it queues for the next payout cycle.

4

You Receive Payment

Payouts are sent on the 15th of each month for all commissions that cleared the 30-day hold by the end of the previous month. (Example: a sale on January 5th clears February 4th and pays out March 15th.)

Recurring commissions compound every month. If a Builders Club member you referred stays active, you earn 15% of their subscription fee every single month they remain subscribed — for up to 11 additional months after the initial payment. One referral can turn into $160+ in backend income over a year.

Affiliate FAQ

How long does my affiliate cookie last?
365 days. If someone clicks your link today and buys 11 months from now, you still get credit — as long as they don't click another affiliate's link in the meantime. This is one of the longest cookies in the industry.
What happens if a customer refunds?
If a refund is issued within the 30-day hold period, the commission is voided before it pays out. If a refund happens after you've been paid, the commission is deducted from your next payout. This is standard across affiliate programs and protects against refund abuse.
Can I promote Business Launchpad through paid ads?
Yes, with prior approval. Reach out in the affiliate community or email affiliates@fasttrackgenai.com with your ad copy, landing page, and platform. We'll review and approve within 48 hours. Unapproved paid ads violate the terms and may result in account suspension.
Do I earn on upsells and recurring subscriptions?
Yes, across the board. You earn your tier rate on every product a referred customer buys in the funnel — Business Launchpad (intro or standard), the 3-Month or 6-Month Fast Passes (if offered as OTO), and Builders Club initial + 15% recurring for up to 12 months. One referral can generate $200+ in lifetime value.
How do I move up commission tiers?
Tiers are cumulative and permanent. Tier 1 (25%) starts at approval. Hit 10 sales in any rolling 30-day window → Tier 2 (30%). Hit 25 sales → Tier 3 (35%). Once you reach a tier, you stay there — even if your monthly volume drops. All future sales earn at your highest unlocked rate.
What promotional materials do I get?
Your affiliate dashboard Resources section includes: email swipe copy (3 templates), social post templates (LinkedIn, X, Facebook), a demo clip library (15sec, 30sec, 60sec video snippets), image assets (comparison graphics, product shots), and a "Quick Start" script you can record as a Loom or voice note. Everything is editable — customize it in your voice.
Can I promote to my email list or community?
Absolutely. Email, Slack groups, Discord servers, private communities, LinkedIn posts, X threads — if it's your audience and you're not spamming strangers, you're good. Just make sure any promotional content accurately represents the product and doesn't make income claims we don't support.

How the Funnel Works & Why Your 365-Day Cookie Matters

When someone clicks your affiliate link, a 365-day tracking cookie is set. That means if they come back 11 months later and buy, you still get credit — as long as they haven't clicked another affiliate's link in the meantime.

Here's how the funnel flows and where you earn at each step:

Entry point. Your link sends people to the Business Launchpad sales page. They can buy at the intro OTO price ($37) or come back later for the standard price ($97). You earn your tier rate on whichever they choose.

One link. Multiple products. Compounding commissions. You don't need separate links for each offer — your single affiliate URL tracks the entire customer journey. If they buy Launchpad today and upgrade to Builders Club next week, both commissions go to you.

Why 365 Days Matters

Most affiliate programs use 30- or 60-day cookies. Ours is a full year. That means someone who's "not ready yet" can sit on your link for months, revisit when the timing's right, and you still earn. It rewards patient, trust-building promotion over high-pressure tactics.

Last-Click Attribution

If a prospect clicks your link, then clicks another affiliate's link before buying, the other affiliate gets credit. First click doesn't win — last click does. This is industry-standard and keeps the system fair.

Cross-Device Tracking

Our tracking persists across browsers and devices as long as the prospect is logged into the same account or email. If they click your link on mobile and buy on desktop three days later, you're covered.
