How & When You Get Paid

Commissions are paid on Net-30 terms — the standard payout schedule built into our affiliate platform. Here's exactly how it works.

Every commission is held ("Pending") until 30 days after the end of the month it was earned in — then it's released to you.

Example: A sale on July 10 is earned in July. July ends on the 31st, the 30-day clock runs through August 30, and your commission releases at the end of August.

In practice that's a 30–60 day wait from the sale date. This window clears our 30-day money-back guarantee before a penny is paid, so you're only ever paid on sales that truly stick.

Recurring works the same way: each monthly Builders Club payment starts its own Net-30 clock when collected — a steady rolling income stream for as long as your referral stays subscribed.

Fair and transparent: if a member refunds while a commission is still pending, it's voided (you're never paid on returned money). Everything already released is yours to keep.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly do I promote?
One simple funnel. You drive traffic to the Fast Track GenAI Launchpad ($37 intro / $97 standard). The GenAI Builders Club ($47/mo intro / $97/mo standard) is presented automatically as the next step in the same session — so a single referral link earns you commissions on both products.
How do the recurring commissions work?
For every Builders Club member you refer, you earn a flat 15% on each monthly payment they make, for up to 12 months. That's up to $194 from a single $97/mo referral across the first year. This is a maximum potential — members can cancel anytime, and you're paid only on payments actually collected.
How long does my referral cookie last?
A full 365 days. If someone clicks your link today and purchases anytime within the next year, you get credit — even if they don't buy on their first visit. This is far more generous than the typical 30–90 day window.
When and how do I get paid?
Commissions are paid on Net-30 terms — held until 30 days after the end of the month they're earned in (a 30–60 day window from the sale). This clears our 30-day money-back guarantee so payouts only ever go out on sales that stick. Recurring commissions release the same way, every month.
Why are these called "founder's rates"?
We're in our launch phase, so we're offering the most generous commissions we ever will. As our brand and YouTube presence grow and traffic gets easier to convert, these rates will come down for new affiliates. But your rate is locked for the life of your partnership — get in now and keep it.
Do I have to hit a sales quota before I can promote everything?
No. There are no gates or tiers to unlock. Every approved affiliate promotes the full funnel and earns on both products — initial and recurring — from day one. Your commission rate simply rises as your total volume grows.
What happens if my referral cancels or refunds?
Future recurring commissions simply stop from the cancellation date. If a refund happens while a commission is still pending, that commission is voided — you're never paid on money that gets returned. Anything already released and past its window is yours to keep.
